The Wizard's Code of Privacy

1. Magical Tracking

This map uses your device's GPS to reveal your location to other authorized wizards. To provide a seamless magical experience, your coordinates are streamed in real-time to our enchanted Firestore database even when the app is in the background or your screen is off.

2. The Wax Seal (Encryption)

All Personal Identifiable Information (PII), including your Name, Magical Handle, and exact Coordinates, are protected with advanced AES-GCM encryption before they ever leave your device. Only those with the proper magical permissions can decrypt this data.

3. The Trusted Circle

Your location is NOT public. Only wizards you have explicitly added to your 'Trusted Marauders' list can see your footprints on their map. You have full control over who is in this circle.

4. Mischief Managed

Location tracking remains active as long as the map is 'unsealed' (a persistent notification will be visible in your system drawer). To stop tracking and instantly wipe your current coordinates from the database, you must select 'Mischief Managed' within the app. We do not maintain a permanent historical record of your past travels.

5. Magical Identity

We use Firebase Authentication to ensure your identity remains secure. You can choose a unique handle to represent yourself, keeping your true identity as secret as you wish.
