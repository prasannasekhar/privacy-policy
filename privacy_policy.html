Privacy Policy for PathScribe

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in PathScribe.

Information We Collect

PathScribe collects and processes the following types of data:

  • Location Data: We collect your precise location data to track your paths, even when the app is closed or not in use (background location).
  • Health Data (Physical Activity): We collect your physical activity data (such as your current activity type like walking, running, or driving) to contextualize your tracked paths and provide meaningful statistics.

How We Use Your Information

We use your Location Data and Health Data exclusively to display your recorded paths on a map and to provide you with insights and statistics about your journeys. Your data is processed locally on your device to ensure privacy. We do not transmit, sell, or share your Location or Health Data with any external servers or third parties.

Data Retention

All data collected by PathScribe, including your Location and Health Data, is stored locally and securely within the app on your device. We retain this data indefinitely to allow you to review your historical paths, unless you explicitly choose to delete it.

Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can permanently delete all your Location and Health Data at any time by navigating to the app's settings and selecting the option to clear or delete your data. Additionally, uninstalling the PathScribe application from your device will permanently delete all associated data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
